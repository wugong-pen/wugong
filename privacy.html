  • 網站運作：登入工作階段、操作與寄信紀錄，以及網站服務處理的連線資訊，用於維持服務、安全、防止濫用及查核異常。

We process account and contact details, order and delivery information, payment references and status, support conversations and warranty records to provide membership, fulfil purchases, verify payment, arrange delivery and provide after-sales service. Authorised personnel use data as needed for their work. Processing includes electronic storage, order handling, email, support, payment and delivery. Processing locations include Taiwan, the delivery destination and provider service locations. 保存期間 / Retention

會員服務期間及處理交易、配送、保固、維修、爭議、安全與法定保存義務所需期間，會保留相應資料。用途消失或依法應停止利用／刪除時，依適用規定處理；如因法律義務或爭議仍須保存，將限制於相關目的使用。瀏覽器儲存可由您清除，清除不會一併刪除伺服器上的訂單。

個人資料刪除及保存必要性的查核由客服受理；請來信說明申請項目，我們會依資料類型、尚未完成的服務及適用法定義務確認處理範圍。

Records are retained while needed for membership, transactions, delivery, warranty, repairs, disputes, security and applicable legal obligations. When no longer needed, or where the law requires restriction or deletion, requests and records will be handled accordingly. Clearing browser storage does not delete server-side orders. Contact customer service to request deletion or review of continued retention. The scope is assessed according to the data type, outstanding services and applicable legal obligations.

您的權利與申請方式 / Your rights

您可聯絡客服申請查詢、閱覽、製給複製本、補充或更正、停止蒐集／處理／利用，以及刪除個人資料。我們會先以必要且適當的方式核對身分，依適用法令處理；若有依法不能立即停止或刪除的資料，將說明理由。會員中心可修改基本資料，目前沒有一鍵刪除帳號功能，請以 Email 提出申請。

Contact customer service to request access, a copy, correction, restriction of collection, processing or use, or deletion of your personal data. Appropriate identity checks may be required. Requests are handled under applicable law; reasons will be explained if records must still be retained. Basic details can be edited in the member centre. Account deletion requests are currently handled by email.

是否提供資料 / Your choice

您可選擇是否提供資料。缺少註冊必填資料時，無法完成會員註冊；缺少必要的收件與付款資訊時，無法完成訂單或配送。訂單留言、活動電子報，以及會員的常用電話與地址為選填；不訂閱活動電子報不影響購物與必要通知。

You may choose whether to provide information. Required registration fields are necessary to create an account; necessary delivery and payment details are needed to process an order. Order messages, event newsletters and saved profile phone/address details are optional. Declining event newsletters does not affect shopping or essential notices.
